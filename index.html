AirDraw

Hold your phone like a magic wand. Draw a symbol in the air. Your phone does the magic.

Download for Android

Free. Android 8.0 or newer. Not on the Play Store, so you install it from this page.

Try the magic

Draw a Circle, Z, C, W, V or L with your finger or mouse. The real app does this with your phone movement, no screen touching.

Draw something...

This demo is a toy with fixed shapes. In the app you teach your own symbols.

How it works

  1. Trigger. Shake the phone 3 times, or hold Volume Down.
  2. Draw. Move your wrist for about 1.5 seconds. The gyroscope tells the app how fast you turn.
  3. 3D to 2D. The app adds up the turning speed into left/right and up/down angles. That gives a flat path, like an air mouse. It does not use the accelerometer for the path, because that drifts away quickly.
  4. Recognize. The path is compared with the symbols you taught (the $1 Unistroke method). If the score is high enough, the action runs.
  5. Feel it. A buzz when it starts, two pulses on success, one long soft buzz for no match.

Symbols and actions

You choose what each symbol does. Examples:

CircleOpen the camera
ZFlashlight on/off
COpen WhatsApp
WCall a saved number
VPlay / pause music
LOpen a web page

Available actions: open any app, flashlight, camera, call a number, open a URL, media play/pause. Any shape works, you record 3 samples of it.

Install

  1. Tap Download for Android. If Chrome warns that the file may be harmful, tap Keep or Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded AirDraw.apk and tap Install. If Android asks, allow installs from Chrome.
  3. If you see "App blocked to protect your device", see the fix below.
  4. Open AirDraw. Tap Teach new symbol and record 3 samples.
  5. Turn the AirDraw ON switch. Shake 3 times (or hold Volume Down), feel the buzz, then draw.
  6. For the Volume Down trigger, turn on AirDraw in Settings, Accessibility.

Problems and fixes

"App blocked to protect your device" (Play Protect)
  1. Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Play Protect.
  2. Tap the gear icon and turn off "Scan apps with Play Protect".
  3. Install AirDraw.
  4. Turn the setting back on.
Accessibility switch is greyed out ("Restricted setting")
  1. Go to Settings, Apps, AirDraw.
  2. Tap the 3-dot menu at the top right.
  3. Tap "Allow restricted settings".
  4. Go back to Accessibility and turn AirDraw on.
After installing an update, nothing works

Turn the AirDraw Accessibility service OFF and then ON again.

"App not installed" when updating

This is a debug build, and each new build has a different signature. Uninstall the old AirDraw first, then install the new one. You will need to teach your symbols again.

Permission popups do not appear

If any app draws over the screen (like a floating bubble), Android blocks permission popups. Stop that app first. Or give the permission by hand in Settings, Apps, AirDraw, Permissions.

It stops working after a while

Some phones kill background services. Open Settings, Apps, AirDraw, Battery, and choose Unrestricted. The AirDraw Settings screen also has a Battery settings button.

Shake does not trigger, or triggers by accident

Open AirDraw Settings and change Shake strength. Lower is easier, higher is harder.

Apps or calls do not open when another app is on screen

Android 10 and newer limits starting apps from the background. Keep the AirDraw Accessibility service turned on, since it usually fixes this. As a backup, allow the Overlay permission from AirDraw Settings.

Symbols get confused or rejected

Draw big, slow wrist movements. Hold the phone the same way you did when teaching. Record the 3 samples in that same posture. Use the Practice screen to see your scores, then adjust the match threshold.

Honest limits

  • Some phones stop sensors when the screen is off. Test it on yours.
  • The path comes from the gyroscope and drifts a little, so only about 1.5 seconds is used.
  • Accuracy depends on how you move. Shapes will vary a bit each time.
  • Opening apps from the background depends on your Android version.
  • Calling a number dials at once. Keep the match threshold high for that symbol.
  • Accessibility apps get flagged by Play Protect and cannot easily go on the Play Store, so this page is the way to install.
